# Optional Office engine qualification

## Distribution

The 1.8.0 GitHub APK ships the Java bridge and its corresponding source and notices. It omits the optional native Office engine and font bundle.

For the separate Office prototype, the [candidate 15 qualification record](evidence/m35-product15-qualification.json) identifies the prepared materials and their hashes. Its distribution status was `VERIFIED_LOCAL_PREPARATION`. The manifest covered 65,696 files and had SHA-256 `9a1ca54fbd03cf51be996008f6ccf32533ec50fa114270ac34655ac32a031712`. The record covers a local review of that candidate.
